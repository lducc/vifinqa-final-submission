# Gold-set policy

The internal 233-question benchmark and its table verdicts are published here
at the owner's request for teammate review. They are development annotations,
not organizer ground truth, and must not be used for leaderboard tuning or
represented as an official test set.

The local files are:

```text
data/annotations/benchmark_v2/benchmark_v2.jsonl
data/annotations/benchmark_v2/verdicts.jsonl
data/annotations/benchmark_v2/manifest.json
```

Their hashes and limitations are documented in
[`benchmark-v2-20260824.md`](benchmark-v2-20260824.md). After the competition
closes, retain them only if the organizer's redistribution terms permit it.
